Our Story

A home, not a venue.

Built nearly a century ago in the quiet lanes of Kilpauk, Harley's House has weathered generations — its red-oxide floors worn smooth, its wooden beams still holding the ceilings they were cut for.

We've kept it a home rather than polished it into a hotel. The bougainvillea still spills over the terrace wall, the lanterns still glow at dusk, and you slow down a little when you climb the stairs.

Today it opens its doors two ways — a boutique stay for those passing through Chennai, and an intimate setting for the celebrations that deserve somewhere with a soul.
