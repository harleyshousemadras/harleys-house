About the House

Step into the timeless elegance of Harley's House — where history, warmth, and comfort meet.

Nestled in a heritage setting, Harley's House whispers stories of a bygone era while wrapping you in modern comfort. With soaring ceilings, vintage accents, and an expansive living area that glows with old-world charm, this is more than just a stay — it's an experience.

The home features a beautifully appointed bedroom and a spacious bathroom, while the grand living area transforms with ease, offering convertible beds that make it perfect for couples, families, or friends traveling together. Every corner is designed to transport you back in time, yet with thoughtful touches to ensure your stay is nothing short of delightful.

Whether you're gathering around for laughter and conversation, unwinding after a day of exploration, or simply soaking in the heritage ambiance, Harley's House welcomes you with a character and grace that's rare to find.

Old-world charm. Modern comfort.
A home for every heart.

Come and experience it.
